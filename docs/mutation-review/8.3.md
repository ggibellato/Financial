## Gaps closed (40 test cases)

- **Portfolio holdings** (29):
  - Every sortable header sorts the rows ascending and marks itself as the active sort (18
    headers, the historic-only ones included); none of the sort keys had a test. A holding with no
    credit month sorts last by last month's credits, whatever stale amount it carries.
  - A row whose price failed shows a dash in the current value, current price and profit, even with
    stored figures; a manual price that is still loading or failed shows no Manual badge; the price
    cell is named Sold price in the historic scope; the last month's credit percent is formatted.
  - The totals: none without holdings; no "pending prices" footnote while calculating or with no
    price at all; the realized (shares only) total is the exact sum. The existing totals tests
    matched `30[.,]00`, which a subtraction's `-30.00` also matches, so they now compare the exact
    figure, and the pending total no longer counts a loading row's value.
  - No table while the holdings reload or fail, even with items from before.
- **Asset summary** (6): the ISIN and local type, and None when either is missing; the current
  price and the current value plus credits; a dash under As of for a price with no date; the
  historic XIRR figures coloured by their own sign. The existing result and XIRR colour tests now
  also check the with-credits figures.
- **Price history** (5): each kind of point drawn with its own marker shape (circle, square, up and
  down triangles), so they differ by more than colour, and a point without coordinates drawn as
  nothing; the chart keeps Recharts' keyboard layer off, as in [8.2](8.2.md); the chart follows a
  newly added price; the Price and Source headers mark the active sort. The chart mock now records
  the keyboard layer and the number of points, and renders the tooltip once with an empty payload.
