# 2.1 Investment.Domain entities (82.0% → 88.1%)

Slice notes for [the mutation review](mutation-review.md). The tracker table row and log line
are updated in a separate tracker PR.

| before | after | added | removed | equivalent | accepted | unreachable | wording | initializers | from Infrastructure |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 82.0% (564 of 688) | 88.1% (606 of 688) | 28 | 0 | 8 | 0 | 7 | 46 | 13 | 8 |

Files: `Entities/**` (`Asset` holds most of the logic). The undetected mutants are dominated by
exception wording and `= string.Empty` property initializers that every factory overwrites, so
the score stays below the Application and Infrastructure slices even with the real gaps closed.

Gaps closed:

- **Disposal regeneration boundaries in `Asset`**: the existing tests are named "at or before the
  latest disposal" but only used earlier dates. Now covered: a buy, an edit and a deletion on the
  disposal date itself; a buy between two disposals regenerates the later one (the latest date is
  the maximum, not the minimum); moving a buy across a disposal regenerates it in both directions
  (the anchor is the earlier of the old and new dates); revise and retract return `true`; a
  backdated buy is added once.
- **Corporate actions in `Asset`**: moving a split across a disposal regenerates it in both
  directions; moving a split to a date with no position is refused; a split rejected by a later
  specific-ID sale, or a retraction such a sale depends on, leaves the position as it was (the
  rollback re-syncs the replay); a null revision throws; a split cannot be revised to a date after
  a merger closed the position (the check uses the other corporate actions, not the split's own old
  version); `PositionAsOf` on a split's date includes the split; a merger target recorded with
  investments but no broker currency gets no tax classification; a retraction whose regeneration
  fails with an unexpected error rethrows it unchanged and keeps the split.
- **`Transactions.Add`**: a buy dated before an existing split is replayed under it, with one split
  after the buy and with splits on both sides (the append shortcut skips the replay, so it may only
  apply when every corporate action is on or before the new transaction; found by the simplify
  review, which showed two of these mutants were not equivalent).
- **`Asset`**: `RealizedGainLossSharesOnly` sums every active disposal; a tax classification is
  found by its source id, not just its type; removing a credit by an empty id throws.
- **`Investments`**: `GetBroker` falls back to the other scope's record when the preferred one has
  none (the existing theory now covers an active-only broker too); any of
  several active brokers is active; a historic broker with portfolios cannot be deleted; a tax rule
  ending where an open-ended one starts does not overlap; a final classification on a historic
  broker blocks deleting its tax rule; a rule applies only to its own category.
- **`Credit`**: each deduction must share the value's sign on its own, even when the combined
  total fits; a deduction equal to the value is allowed; the shares-for-dividend backfill validates
  its input.
- **`CorporateAction`**: zero cash in lieu is allowed. **`TaxRule`**: a null description is stored
  as empty on create and update.

The 82 still undetected, all reviewed:

- **Wording** (46): exception messages, plus the choice between two message texts (sale coverage,
  split vs convert) and the order of tax years inside a message.
- **Property initializers** (13): `= string.Empty` on properties every factory and the
  deserializer set.
- **Tested from Infrastructure** (8): the private collection setters the JSON deserializer uses
  (`Asset` corporate actions, credits, price snapshots; `EntityGuard.ReplaceAll`), and
  `ICollection<Transaction>.IsReadOnly`, which System.Text.Json reads when it fills the
  collection; covered by the Investment.Infrastructure serializer tests.
- **Unreachable** (7): `ReviseTransaction`/`RetractTransaction` returning `false` after the lookup
  already found the transaction (2); `Transactions.Update`'s empty-id guard (a `Transaction` id is
  never empty, its constructor replaces `Guid.Empty`) (1); the explicit `ICollection<Transaction>.Contains` and
  `Remove`, which nothing calls (4).
- **Equivalent** (8): the realized-gain refresh after a backfilled disposal (every caller refreshes
  again after its loop); `<` vs `<=` when picking the earlier of two equal dates (2); clamping fees
  and withheld at `< 0` vs `<= 0` (2: zero stays zero); `<=` vs `<` in the `Transactions.Add`
  append shortcut (a corporate action on the same date replays before the transaction anyway);
  `value >= 0` in `Credit` (zero is rejected earlier); trimming a whitespace-only local type code
  (it trims to empty anyway).
