## Gaps closed (13 test cases)

- **Credits tab** (7):
  - The chart colours: a single credit type is drawn in the single-type blue, several are shaded
    from light to dark blue, and the bars stack together only while Stacked is chosen. The recharts
    mock now records each bar's fill and stack.
  - The line chart's tooltip formats numbers to two decimals, like the bar chart's.
  - A new credit sends the withheld amount and intermediation fee typed (only the default zeros
    were sent before).
  - Clicking the Type and Value headers marks them as the active sort (added to the existing table
    of headers).
- **Transactions tab** (6): clicking the Type, Quantity, Unit price, Fees, Withheld and Net headers
  marks each as the active sort.

## Still undetected

All reviewed:

- **Equivalent** (4): the credit palette for no types (with none there are no bars to colour, so
  the guard, its boundary and its placeholder colour do not show) and a palette one colour longer
  than the types (the extra colour is never read).
- **Accepted** (25):
  - Chart props (21): the recharts margins, tick font sizes, label style, dot radius and the
    `accessibilityLayer` switch on the bar and line charts of both tabs. The tests mock recharts,
    and these change only how the chart is drawn.
  - The compact chart panel class (4) on both tabs, styling only.
- **Wording** (1): the hint under the credit value.
