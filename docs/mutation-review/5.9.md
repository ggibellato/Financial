## Gaps closed (19 test cases)

- **Income tab**: loading shows from the first render; a new income opens with no bank, even with a
  bank remembered from an earlier save; deleting the income being edited closes its form, and
  deleting another income keeps it. The accounts fixture is now out of name order, so the existing
  test checks the banks are sorted.
- **Tithe**: the detail and the history show loading from the first render; the history loads again
  for another year.
- **Balance adjustment**: an adjustment with no note opens with an empty note; a date of spaces is
  refused as missing; a note of spaces is sent as no note; a remembered bank with no balance is not
  pre-selected; the bank's display name follows the chosen bank and is empty for an unknown one. The
  negative balance test now checks the message.
- **Settlement entry**: settling a currency that is already settled leaves who pays and the amounts
  empty; the outcome of paying part of what you owe; no implied rate without an amount paid; the
  paid currency is quoted when both values are equal; the description is sent without surrounding
  spaces; a converted value of spaces is accepted and sent as none.
- **Statement due date**: the first and last day of the month are accepted; a save that succeeds
  after the form was reopened on another month leaves that form alone (the existing test covered
  only a failure).
