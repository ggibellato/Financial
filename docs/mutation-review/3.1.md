# 3.1 CashFlow.Application accounts (70.3% → 80.5%)

Slice notes for [the mutation review](mutation-review.md). The tracker table row and log line
are updated in a separate tracker PR.

| before | after | added | removed | equivalent |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 70.3% (353 of 502) | 80.5% (404 of 502) | 9 | 29 | 21 |

Files: `AccountAdminService`, `AccountGroupSummaryService`, `AccountQueryService`,
`AccountTransferService`, `BalanceAdjustmentService`, `AccountReferenceReader`,
`AccountTransactionReader`. About 1,600 lines of services against 4,200 lines of tests.

Gaps closed:

- **Spans** with the entity id on every account, transfer and adjustment write and on the
  account, transaction, statement and counterpart reads; failed spans for the cards-in-credit
  read, a failed adjustment delete and null requests.
- **Null requests** on every write method throw `ArgumentNullException` before any lookup.
- **Delete refused when a bank is used** by an income, an expense paid from it, or a transfer in
  either direction (the `AccountReferenceReader` branches no test reached).
- **Group totals** add the round-ups of every member in a currency and the gross values of every
  income from a source (one member or one gross value could not tell a sum from a pick).
- **Transactions**: a record on the last day of the month belongs to that month; an income into
  an inactive bank cannot be edited.
- **Statements**: one paid by a transfer that no longer exists shows as paid with no payment
  date; payable statements sort by year before month (a December of the previous year now comes
  first).

Removed, after the simplify review's altitude check (`Financial.CashFlow.Domain.Tests` owns these
rules, or another test in the file covers the same path):

- 9 `AccountQueryServiceTests`: empty and settled-charge statements, the future-month viewed
  statement, inactive-card and inactive-settlement actions, the bank without refund-to-bank, the
  two-month total owed, the card without a dated statement, and the no-pots page
  (`CardStatementLedgerTests`, `AccountActionsTests`, `CardDueDateTests`, `CardChargeFilterTests`).
- 2 `AccountGroupSummaryServiceTests`: a paid card member (contained in a stronger test) and a
  template without listed accounts (`DefaultAccountGroupTests`).
- 5 `AccountAdminServiceTests`: name and other-participant length, negative opening balance
  (`AccountTests`, `OpeningBalanceTests`), the settlement-with-an-entry template change (same path
  as the currency change), and a currency change that only re-checked the bank's GBP rule.
- 7 `AccountTransferServiceTests`: cross/same-currency destination amounts, a refund to a bank in
  another currency, a card owing money, the same-currency update (`TransferTests`,
  `CardChargeFilterTests`), the statement-payment delete (same branch as the ordinary transfer),
  and the single `AddAsync` null-request test, folded into the per-file one.
- 6 `BalanceAdjustmentServiceTests`: the span-only add test (merged into the add test), the
  span-only unknown-account update (merged into its twin), negative target and unreconcilable
  account (`BalanceAdjustmentTests`, `CapabilityGuardTests`), the same-date balance check
  (`AccountBalanceRuleTests`), and the unknown-id update (killed nothing the other-account test
  does not). The file's narrative comments went too.

The 98 still undetected, all reviewed:

- **Log and exception wording** (75).
- **Equivalent** (21): `ConfigureAwait` (11); the template pre-check applying currencies to a
  preview (the real create repeats it); `First` on groups and balances that are never empty (3);
  the statement-only branches for due date, viewed statement and card charge rows (3: an account
  without statements gets nothing from them anyway); a zero-value charge (the Domain rejects it);
  the in-place `UpdateTransfer` and `UpdateBalanceAdjustment` calls (2: the entity is already
  changed in place).
- **Unreachable** (2): the catch around the static account template list.

Production note (not changed in a test PR): `BalanceAdjustmentService.AddAdjustmentAsync` sets the
span's entity id to the account id, while update and delete use the adjustment id and other
services' adds use the new entity's id. The test pins today's behaviour.
