# 1.1 CashFlow.Domain entities (90.3% → 94.5%)

are updated in a separate tracker PR.

|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 90.3% (602 of 667) | 94.5% (630 of 667) | 18 | 0 | 10 | 1 | 4 | 10 | 12 |

Files: `Entities/**`. Timeouts are counted as detected.

Gaps closed:

- **`Expense` round-up suggestion** (the batch-0 trial's lead: `SuggestedRoundUpAmount` had one
  test, for the not-offered case): offered for an immediate payment from a round-up bank; not
  offered once a round-up is set, for a card charge or a settled card expense, or for a
  reimbursement.
- **`Expense`**: a card charge on an inactive card is refused; a settled expense keeps its invoice
  date when its date changes and no invoice date is requested.
- **`Account`**: changing a settlement account's currencies keeps its other participant (setup
  changes re-apply the capability defaults; found by the simplify review); a new bank starts with
  a zero opening balance and round-up off; null capabilities, currencies, pot, pot changes and category are rejected naming the parameter (for
  pot changes, LINQ would otherwise throw its own `ArgumentNullException` for `source`).
- **`Income`**: a zero net value is allowed; an update needs an income source; moving the split to
  an inactive pots account is refused.
- **`InvestmentAccount`**: an update naming a pots account for a non-pots source is refused; an
  account cannot be linked to an inactive card.
- **Range edges**: a recurring bill's due day 1 and 31, and a tithe carry-forward's months 1 and 12,
  are allowed.
- **`BalanceAdjustment`** contributes nothing to another account; **`SettlementTransaction`** rejects
  a null account in each of its static and instance helpers.

The 37 still undetected, all reviewed:

- **Wording** (10): exception messages.
- **Initializers** (12): `= string.Empty` on properties every factory sets, and
  `CountsAsTithe = true` (the factory passes the value; the initializer only matters for a stored
  record without the key).
- **From Infrastructure** (4): `SettlementTransaction.EnsureConsistentWithAccount`, which only the
  CashFlow data file loader calls.
- **Unreachable** (1): `ItemCollection`'s non-generic `GetEnumerator`.
- **Equivalent** (10): three capability defaults (the opening balance and round-up only belong to
  a bank, which has a single allowed currency, so a setup change from bank to bank returns before
  re-applying them and a new account has neither set yet; `Currencies` returns `[Currency]` for a
  single-currency account whatever is stored); `Single` vs `SingleOrDefault` for a pot change
  (both fail on a missing id); the early `return` in `IdCollection.Replace` (ids are unique);
  `PayerOf(0)` (amounts are always positive); `<=` vs `<` against a zero expense value (3: a zero
  value is rejected on create); `ListingDate`'s charge-date fallback (a card charge always has an
  invoice date, a bank expense neither).
