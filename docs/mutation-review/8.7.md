## Gaps closed (36 test cases added, 4 extended)

- **Move asset dialog** (15 added): only this broker's named portfolios are offered when the tree
  holds other brokers first, and whether the source emptied is read from this broker's own source;
  a tree that cannot be read after the move still reports it; while the portfolios load nothing is
  offered, a new portfolio is the choice, its name is blank and archiving is not offered yet; a
  failed load says so; another asset reads the portfolios again; Historic is not read for a closed
  asset unless archiving is offered; a drop on a broker offers only a new portfolio; the existing
  and new choices switch and enable only their own field; a closed asset starts on keeping it
  active and Keep brings the active portfolios back; Move is disabled while it runs, a failed move
  is named, can be tried again and its message clears while it runs; a dropped asset moves once
  even when the source empties; Delete and Keep are disabled while the emptied source is deleted,
  and a failed tidy-up is named and can be tried again.
- **Corporate actions tab** (9 added, 2 extended): a split's ratio drops only trailing zeros
  (×2, ×1.5, ×10); a merger or spin-off record without its role shows no resulting change, and a
  merger never reads as a split; an unknown spin-off quantity says units were received; the
  affected asset is blank while the asset is unknown; a record asked for is focused when it is the
  only one and once the history finishes loading, scrolled to the centre, highlighted alone, for
  two seconds, and a record asked for next keeps its own two seconds; the Type header marks its
  sort; rows take focus without joining the tab order; a record asked for that is not loaded is
  ignored before its id is used to look up a row, so an id with a quote does not break the tab.
- **Corporate action form** (4 added, 1 extended): every field reports its change with its key;
  Type is locked while saving and on the merger confirm step, and the fields step keeps it open
  with Note and Cancel rather than Back; the spin-off preview reads as one sentence and names the
  new asset generically until chosen; a percentage outside 0 to 100 and a negative exchange ratio preview
  nothing; the merger summary reads as one sentence.
- **Disposals tab** (8 added, 1 extended): the audit trail is named by its title and controlled by
  its toggle; a disposal marked expanded without history shows none; a gain is green and a loss
  red in the sale and its audit trail; each sortable header marks its sort.

## Still undetected

All reviewed:

- **Equivalent** (19):
  - The move dialog's tree lookups: a broker's siblings and a portfolio's siblings are always
    brokers and portfolios, so dropping the node type checks changes nothing (3).
  - Fallbacks the dialog never shows (5): the Historic list before it loads, the chosen
    portfolio's starting value and its reset on switching scope, the empty Historic list for an
    open position, and the empty choice with nothing to choose. They differ only for a portfolio
    literally named like the mutant, or for props changed while the modal is open, which no caller
    does.
  - The existing-portfolio message and its check (2): reached only when there are portfolios to
    choose, and then one is always chosen.
  - The select's own "no destinations" check (1), already part of choosing a new portfolio.
  - The submit guard (3): Move is disabled with no destination or while saving, and a drop always
    names its destination.
  - The source count fallback and the failed lookup's catch (2): `-1`, `1` and `undefined` all
    read as not empty.
  - The dialog's open change handler (1): Fluent reports only closing for a dialog held open.
  - The corporate action form's confirm step for a non-merger (1): only a merger reaches it.
  - The corporate actions tab's spin-off branch for a split with no ratio (1): splits always have a
    ratio and no role, so it has no role to show either way.
