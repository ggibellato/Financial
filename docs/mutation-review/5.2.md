Files: `useCorporateActions` (62.8% → 89.6%) and `useAccountActionForms` (87.5%, measured only),
the two hooks with the most undetected mutants in the nightly report. Measured with every test
that reaches each hook through its imports (`STRYKER_TEST_INCLUDE`: the two hook tests,
`CorporateActionForm`, `CorporateActionsTab` and `AccountScreenPage`), not every web test, which
keeps a run to about an hour. `useAccountActionForms` scores 52% in the nightly report only
because the account page test is left out there (see [5.1](5.1.md)).

## Gaps closed in `useCorporateActions` (21 tests)

- **Split ratio**: saved as numerator over denominator (the only save test used `2` over `1`, so
  `/` vs `*` survived); a zero numerator is refused; a stored factor of `null` or exactly 1
  reopens as `'' / 1` and `1 / 1`.
- **Optional fields sent as `null` when blank**: whitespace-only target or new-asset ISIN, exchange
  and ticker, cash in lieu and note are sent as `null`, and typed ones as written (both merger and
  spin-off, and the split note).
- **Validation edges**: whitespace-only effective date and target or new asset name; a negative
  cash in lieu; a blank, negative and above-100 allocation are refused, and 0 and 100 accepted.
- **Edit form**: a merger or spin-off being edited loads only its own fields, and a split leaves
  the merger and spin-off fields blank; a new form starts with them blank; cancel closes it.
- **Asset lookups** run only for a new merger or spin-off, not for a split.
- **Load failure** stops loading and shows the load message; deleting a different corporate action
  keeps the edit form open.

## `useAccountActionForms`: deferred to 5.3

Its 38 undetected mutants are in how its ten forms close one another: 14 are `if (form.isOpen)
form.cancel()` guards turned to `true` (equivalent: cancelling a closed form is a no-op), and the
rest are the `false` side of the pay-bill, pay-statement, credit-refund and move forms' guards,
the credit-refund mode label, the form keys used to remount forms, and the row-edit and
operation-delete orchestration. Covering them means driving card, pay-bill and move flows through
the hook, which is a slice of its own.

## The 30 still undetected in `useCorporateActions`, all reviewed

- **Equivalent** (14): the `useCallback` dependency lists (8: `dispatch` keeps its identity); a
  blank cash in lieu skipping the parse (3: a blank value parses to `null` anyway); `>=` vs `>` 1
  for the ratio display (a factor of exactly 1 reopens as `1 / 1` either way); the retry counter's
  `+` vs `-` (any change refetches); deleting while editing, reported as surviving although
  replacing that condition with `true` by hand fails the "different corporate action deleted" test
  (a coverage quirk in the report).
- **Unreachable** (7): `?? null` for the first error message (3: the dispatch only runs with at
  least one error); the linked asset name fallback for a merger or spin-off (2: the server always
  sends it); the "saved but could not refresh" branches (2: no test triggers a refresh failure
  after a save).
- **Wording** (6): the save and delete failure messages.
- **Not pinned** (3): `findAssetByName`'s preference between the two sides of a merger or spin-off
  result when picking the asset to show.
