# Privacy policy

Financial is a personal, self-hosted finance tool. Each install is run by one person on their own computer, for their own use. There is no hosted service, no user accounts and no analytics.

## Google Calendar

When you connect Google Calendar, the app asks for access to your calendars so it can:

- list your calendars, so you can pick the one to use;
- create, update, read and delete the events it manages, such as credit card payment reminders.

The app does not read, change or share any other calendar data.

## Where your data goes

- The Google Calendar tokens are stored in a local file on the computer where you run the app.
- Your financial data stays in the install's own data files, on that computer or in your own Google Drive if you choose that storage option.
- Calendar data is sent only between your install and Google's APIs.
- Nothing is sent to the app's author or to any third party.

## Revoking access

Disconnect the calendar in the app, or remove access for "Financial" at https://myaccount.google.com/permissions.
